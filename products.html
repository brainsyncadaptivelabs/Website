Featured Product

PlacementAI Platform

Intelligent career readiness dashboard and ATS resume matching engine operating on local vector search models.

UNDER DEVELOPMENT Next.js 15 Spring Boot 3 Ollama Llama3 Tesseract OCR SQLite-VSS

PlacementAI solves the resume screening bottleneck and career preparation gaps for universities and enterprises alike. By performing secure local OCR scans of candidate materials, it indexes skill matrices on device nodes, mapping pathways to job descriptions with unmatched precision.

Key Platform Features:

  • Local OCR Pipeline: Scan documents client-side securely with Tesseract bindings, keeping student transcripts and credentials private.
  • ATS Evaluation Matrix: Score resumes against target roles using semantic embeddings computed by local vector engines.
  • Dynamic Placement Analytics: Interactive widgets detailing skill gaps, interview readiness scoring, and customized training recommendations.
  • Off-Grid Execution: Deploys on edge hardware without sending sensitive personal data to external cloud LLM databases.

Featured R&D Product

EGO Platform

Equipment Guardian & Optimize — An upcoming AI-powered Industrial IoT platform for real-time factory operation and equipment diagnostics.

R&D PHASE Industrial IoT Machine Learning Real-Time Analytics Edge Computing Industry 4.0

EGO (Equipment Guardian & Optimize) is an upcoming AI-powered Industrial IoT platform by BrainSync Adaptive Labs designed to help manufacturing industries monitor, analyze, and optimize factory operations in real time. The platform integrates machines, sensors, and production systems into a centralized digital ecosystem, providing complete visibility across the manufacturing process.

Leveraging Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, and real-time analytics, EGO enables production monitoring, predictive maintenance, equipment health tracking, downtime analysis, and intelligent decision-making. The system helps manufacturers improve operational efficiency, reduce unexpected failures, increase productivity, and optimize resource utilization through data-driven insights and automated alerts.

Built for the future of Industry 4.0, EGO aims to transform traditional factories into intelligent, connected, and self-optimizing manufacturing environments. The platform acts as a digital guardian for industrial equipment, continuously monitoring machine health, identifying potential issues before they occur, and optimizing operational performance through intelligent automation and predictive analytics.

EGO is designed to support industries such as automotive manufacturing, pharmaceuticals, food processing, electronics, heavy engineering, and industrial manufacturing, providing scalable solutions for both single-plant and multi-plant operations.

"Intelligent Equipment. Optimized Operations. Smarter Manufacturing."
Vision: Empowering smart manufacturing through AI, Industrial IoT, and intelligent automation.

Key Platform Features:

  • Real-Time Operation Monitoring: Centralize telemetry data from factory-floor machines, IIoT sensors, and controllers onto unified edge gateways.
  • Predictive Maintenance Alerts: Leverage ML algorithms to identify mechanical degradation early, preventing costly unscheduled line stoppages.
  • Downtime Diagnostics: Automatically log operational interruptions, analyze causal pathways, and formulate remedial actions.
  • Smarter Plant Automation: Streamline resource management across single or multi-facility deployments through automated scheduling controls.
