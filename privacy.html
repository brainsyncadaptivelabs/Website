Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

At BrainSync Adaptive Labs, we prioritize your data privacy, particularly regarding product licenses, data integrations, and local AI execution. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is handled, and our commitment to off-grid security.

1. System Telemetry & Performance Data

Our client integrations (utilizing the SyncLink SDK) stream system workload metrics and performance telemetry directly from local database nodes to client dashboard displays.

  • Zero-Cloud Telemetry: All performance metrics and log files are processed locally via WebSockets on your machine. We do not store or transmit raw server metrics or proprietary database entries to our public cloud nodes.
  • Local Customization: Any user layout changes (such as grid density changes in SyncOS) are processed client-side and stored in temporary local variables.

2. Information Collected via Portal

When you request a license or contact our developer intake channels, we collect your name, enterprise details, communication endpoint (email), and scope requirements to verify trial qualifications and distribute authentication keys.

3. Local Database & Index Security

Products like CogTwin utilize local SQLite-VSS vectors database directories. These databases sit completely on-device, fully encrypted in accordance with client system security parameters, with no off-premises indexing.

4. Your Rights

You can request the deletion, correction, or audit of any licensing details stored in our databases at any time. For questions regarding data security, you can contact our privacy officer at privacy@brainsync.com.
