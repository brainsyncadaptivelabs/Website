Cookie Policy

Last Updated: June 10, 2026

To ensure our interfaces adapt correctly and maintain secure sessions during evaluation requests, we utilize standard browser storage and cookies. This policy explains our minimal storage methods.

1. What are Cookies and Local Storage?

Cookies and browser Local Storage are small text blocks saved on your device when you load web pages. They help remember state settings, grid metrics, and dashboard preferences.

2. Cookies We Use

We only deploy strictly necessary and functional cookies. We run zero marketing, advertising, or cross-site tracking systems.

  • Functional Storage: Remembers your chosen theme preferences (such as dashboard layout variables) or interactive workload simulator slider levels (e.g. low system load versus high workload values).
  • Session Security: Standard token values that prevent cross-site request forgery when using our evaluation form.

3. Controlling Storage

You can modify, block, or clear cookies and local storage through your browser settings at any time. Disabling strictly necessary session keys might cause contact form submissions to function incorrectly.

4. Cookie Scope Updates

We may occasionally modify our storage guidelines to reflect changes in edge database indexing or telemetry scripts. The updated policy will be posted on this page with the latest revision date.
